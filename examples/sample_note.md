- Mid-market freight brokerage and warehousing in North America; revenue FY24 $3.1B.

3. **Warehousing mix shift**: contract warehousing (5-yr contracts) grows 9% CAGR, smooths earnings.
4. **Capital return**: $300M buyback authorised (~10% of market cap); net debt/EBITDA 1.1x.
